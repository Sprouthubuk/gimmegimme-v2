This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your rights. We follow UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1. Who We Are

GIMMEGIMMEPRIZES LTD, trading as Gimme Gimme Prizes (company number 17496537), 57 Oakhanger Drive, Bristol, BS11 0RJ, United Kingdom, is the controller of your personal information. For anything about your information, email hello@gimmegimmeprizes.co.uk with "Data" in the subject, or write to us at that address.

2. What We Collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored scrambled, never readable), date of birth and phone number.
  • Address: your postal address, when you give it for prize delivery or a withdrawal.
  • Entries and results: the Competitions you enter, your ticket numbers, how you entered (paid or by post), and anything you win.
  • Payments and your Wallet: your orders, top-ups, withdrawals and Wallet balances, and the payment references, amounts and card type (credit or debit) our payment provider sends us. We never receive or store your full card number or security code.
  • Checks: copies of ID and proof of address you send us for an age, identity or withdrawal check, and your bank details for paying withdrawals.
  • Safer play: your spending limits, breaks and self-exclusions, and any concerns we record about your wellbeing.
  • Postal entries: the details written on your entry and the date and time we received it.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form, by email or by post, and our replies.
  • Technical information: your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you visit, kept in our security logs.
  • Marketing choices: whether you agreed to email or text marketing, and when.
  • Cookie choices: whether you accepted marketing cookies, and when (see our Cookie Policy).

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, information about your health, ethnicity, religion, politics or similar sensitive matters.

4. Automated Decisions

Results are decided by chance by our draw engine, not by any assessment of you. Our systems automatically stop entries from accounts that are under 18, have not confirmed their email, are on a break or self-excluded, or are on hold. If you think one of these has been applied to you by mistake, contact us and a person will look at it.

5. What We Publish

To prove our Competitions are fair, the website shows: each Competition's list of ticket numbers with masked names (for example "L*** O."); winning ticket numbers; and winners' first names and surname initials (for example "Liam O."). Our draw engine's public records identify entries by a scrambled code, never by name. If you would rather not be named, email us and we will show you as "Anonymous". We may have to give a winner's full name and county to the Advertising Standards Authority if it asks.

6. Who We Share It With

We never sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Cashflows, our payment provider, to take card payments and refunds.
  • Our hosting and security providers, who run our servers in the UK and protect the website (DigitalOcean, Laravel Forge and Cloudflare).
  • Our email provider, to send account, receipt and marketing emails.
  • Sentry, which records technical errors on our website so we can fix them. We set it never to receive names, email addresses, IP addresses, payment details or anything you type into a form.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd, only if you accept marketing cookies (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Delivery companies and prize suppliers, to send you a prize you have won.
  • Our bank, to pay your withdrawals.
  • Professional advisers such as our accountants, lawyers and insurers.
  • The police, courts, regulators and fraud prevention agencies, where the law requires it or to prevent fraud or crime.
  • A buyer of our business, if we sell or reorganise it, who would have to protect your information in the same way.

Our service providers may use your information only to provide their service to us, under written contracts.

7. Transfers Outside The UK

Some of our providers (such as Cloudflare, our email provider, Sentry and Meta) may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we make sure it is protected by UK adequacy regulations or the UK's approved data transfer agreements.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to: ask for a copy of your information; have it corrected; have it deleted; restrict or object to how we use it (including objecting to anything based on legitimate interests); receive the information you gave us in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time. Some rights have limits: for example, we must keep certain records by law, and we keep self-exclusion records to protect you.

To use your rights, email hello@gimmegimmeprizes.co.uk. We will reply within one month. We may need to check your identity first.

You can stop marketing at any time in your account settings or with the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Keeping It Safe

We protect your information with encryption in transit, scrambled passwords, two-step sign-in for our staff, access limited to people who need it, and regular backups. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

11. Children

Our website is only for people aged 18 or over. If we find we hold information about someone under 18, we will close the account and delete what we do not need to keep by law.

12. Changes To This Policy

We will update this policy when how we use information changes. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant we will tell you by email or on the website before it applies.
