7. How Results Are Decided

7.1. All results are decided by Verity, an independent computer draw engine that records every step on a tamper-evident audit record. When a Competition is published, Verity fixes a secret value (the seed) and publishes a fingerprint of it, so the seed cannot be changed afterwards.

7.2. Instant wins. In an Instant Win Competition, Verity uses the seed to attach each instant prize to a ticket number before the Competition opens. The winning ticket number for every instant prize is published on the Competition Page from the start, together with whether it has been won. An instant prize is won when its ticket number is issued to an entrant. Prizes whose ticket numbers have not been issued by the Closing Time are not won and are not awarded.

7.3. Draws. A Draw is made by Verity after the Closing Time, combining the seed with a public source of randomness that did not exist when the Competition opened.

7.4. Once a Competition has ended, its seed is published, so anyone can check on the Website that its instant prizes and Draws were fixed in advance and decided as described.

7.5. Every Ticket, paid or free, has an equal chance of winning each prize.

7.6. End prizes. Where an Instant Win Competition has an end prize, it is drawn from every Ticket issued, including Tickets that won an instant prize. Where there is more than one end prize, each is drawn from every Ticket, so a person with more than one Ticket can win more than one end prize.

7.7. Games. In an Instant Win Competition each Ticket plays a game such as a scratch card, a wheel or a claw. The result is fixed when the Ticket is issued; the game only shows it.
