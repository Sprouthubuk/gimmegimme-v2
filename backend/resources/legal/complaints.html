If something has gone wrong, tell us and we will put it right where we can. We acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days and give a full answer within 14 days.

How to complain

Email hello@gimmegimmeprizes.co.uk with "Complaint" in the subject, or write to GIMMEGIMMEPRIZES LTD, 57 Oakhanger Drive, Bristol, BS11 0RJ.

Please include:

  1. Your name and the email address on your account
  2. The competition name, order number or ticket number, if it relates to one
  3. What happened and when
  4. What you would like us to do

What happens next

StepWhat we doWhen
1. AcknowledgeConfirm we have your complaint and who is handling itWithin 2 working days
2. InvestigateCheck our records, payment and draw logs, and talk to you if we need more informationAs soon as possible
3. Final responseExplain what we found and what we will do. If it is complex, we will tell you and reply within 28 daysWithin 14 days
4. ReviewIf you are not happy, ask for a review. A director who was not involved will look at it againWithin 14 days of your request

If you are still unhappy

  • Consumer advice: Citizens Advice consumer helpline, 0808 223 1133.
  • Advertising: the Advertising Standards Authority at asa.org.uk.
  • Your data: the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
  • The courts: you can bring a claim, for example through the small claims process.

We are not currently a member of an alternative dispute resolution (ADR) scheme.

Our commitments

  • Making a complaint will never affect how we treat you or your entries.
  • While a complaint about a competition result is open, we will keep all records relating to it.
  • We keep records of complaints for 6 years to help us improve.
  • This procedure does not affect your statutory rights.
