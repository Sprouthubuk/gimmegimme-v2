The rules

One entry per envelope

Send each entry in its own envelope with a first or second class stamp. Bulk entries in one envelope will not be accepted.

Write it clearly

Put your details on a postcard or a plain piece of paper inside the envelope. Handwritten or printed are both fine.

Get it there in time

Your entry must arrive before the competition closes. Entries that arrive late will not be included in the draw.

You need a free account

Register a free Gimme Gimme account and write the same email address on your entry. If your envelope arrives first we keep it, and enter it as soon as you register with that email, as long as the competition is still open.

Same limits apply

Postal entries count towards the maximum number of tickets per person for that competition, together with any tickets you have bought. Safer play settings apply too: we cannot enter you while you are taking a break or have excluded yourself.

Instant wins included

If the competition has instant wins, your postal ticket is checked for a prize as soon as we enter it, exactly like a paid ticket. Cash prizes go straight into your wallet. We email you, and the win shows on your account next time you sign in.
