The Rules

  • One entry per envelope. Each envelope gives one ticket in one competition. Send each entry in its own envelope with a first or second class stamp. Entries sent by recorded, signed-for or special delivery, or several entries in one envelope, will not be accepted.
  • Write it clearly. Put your details on a postcard or a plain piece of paper inside the envelope, handwritten or printed. Entries we cannot read, or that are missing details, cannot be entered.
  • Get it there in time. Your entry must reach us before the competition's closing time. We record the date and time we receive every envelope. Entries that arrive after the closing time are not entered. Allow at least 5 working days for post.
  • You need a free account. Register a free Gimme Gimme account and write the same email address on your entry. If your envelope arrives before you register, we keep it and enter it as soon as you register with that email, as long as the competition is still open.
  • Enter for yourself only. You can only enter yourself. Entries sent on behalf of other people, or in bulk, are not accepted.
  • Safer play applies. We cannot enter you while you are taking a break or have excluded yourself, or if your account is on hold.
  • Same chance, same ticket. Your entry gets a randomly chosen ticket number exactly like a paid entry, and appears in the competition's entry list.
  • Instant wins included. If the competition has instant wins, your ticket is checked for a prize as soon as we enter it, exactly like a paid ticket. Cash and site credit prizes go straight into your Winnings balance, and the ticket and any win show in your account.
  • Post. We are not responsible for entries lost, delayed or damaged in the post. Proof of posting is not proof of receipt.
