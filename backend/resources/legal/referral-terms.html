These Refer a Friend Terms form part of our Terms & Conditions. If they conflict, these terms apply to the Refer a Friend scheme.

How It Works

  1. Every Gimme Gimme account has its own share link and code, shown in Account → Refer A Friend.
  2. A friend must open your link, or enter your code, when they create their Gimme Gimme account. A code cannot be added to an account afterwards.
  3. The referral qualifies when your friend's first paid ticket order is at least the minimum shown on the Refer A Friend page (£5 at launch). Only money paid by card or from deposit or winnings balances counts. Bonus credit, free tickets, vouchers and free postal entries do not count.
  4. When the referral qualifies, you and your friend each receive the reward shown on the Refer A Friend page at the time your friend joined: free tickets for a live draw of your choice (a voucher), or bonus credit.

Free-Ticket Vouchers

  • A voucher gives the number of tickets shown on it in one live draw you choose from the list offered. Instant win games cannot be chosen.
  • Vouchers expire 30 days after they are given. An expired voucher cannot be used or replaced.
  • Voucher tickets are given random ticket numbers and have the same chance of winning as any other ticket. They count towards any limit on tickets per person.
  • Vouchers have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged.

Bonus Credit

Where the reward is bonus credit, it is added to your bonus balance. Bonus credit can only be spent on tickets, cannot be withdrawn, and expires on the date shown in your wallet.

Limits

  • You can receive referrer rewards for up to 10 friends in each calendar month (UK time). Friends who qualify after that still receive their own reward.
  • You must be 18 or over, live in the UK and meet our Terms & Conditions. Staff and their households cannot take part.
  • You must not refer yourself or create more than one account. Referrals from the same person, household, address, card, device or internet connection are not rewarded.
  • Share your link only with people you know. Do not post it on coupon or voucher websites, send unsolicited messages, or pay for advertising that uses our name.

Checks And Reversals

  • We check every referral. Some are held for a manual review before any reward is given; we may refuse a reward where we reasonably believe these terms have been broken.
  • If the qualifying order is refunded or charged back within 14 days, unused rewards are taken back. Tickets already placed in a draw are not removed.
  • We may change or end the scheme at any time. Rewards already earned will still be honoured unless these terms have been broken.
