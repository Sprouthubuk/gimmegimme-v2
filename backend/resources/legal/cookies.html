This policy explains the cookies and similar storage our website uses. We use as few as we can: the essential ones the website needs to work, and, only if you agree, Meta's marketing cookies.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Websites can also save information in your browser's own storage. We call both "cookies" in this policy.

2. Essential Cookies

These are needed for the website to work, so they are always on. They are only used by us.

Cloudflare, which protects our website, may also set a short-lived essential cookie to tell real visitors from automated attacks.

3. Marketing Cookies (Meta)

Marketing cookies are off until you switch them on in our cookie banner. If you accept them, we use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd) to measure and improve our adverts on Facebook and Instagram. We share: pages you view, competitions you look at, basket and checkout events, purchases and top-ups with their value, account sign-ups, your browser and IP address, and your email address and phone number in hashed (scrambled) form. Meta's cookies (such as _fbp) last up to 3 months.

If you reject marketing cookies we send nothing to Meta, and nothing from Meta loads on our website.

4. Changing Your Choice

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from "Cookie Settings" in the footer of every page. Withdrawing stops all further sharing straight away. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but if you block essential cookies you will not be able to sign in or check out.

For more about how we use your information, see our Privacy Policy.
